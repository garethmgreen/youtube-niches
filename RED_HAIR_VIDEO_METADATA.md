## ✨ KEY FACTS (Load-Bearing Science)

✓ Red hair caused by MC1R gene variants (recessive)  
✓ 1–2% global, 10–13% Scotland, 10% Ireland  
✓ Often co-occurs with fair skin + freckles  
✓ MC1R variants produce pheomelanin (red/yellow) not eumelanin (brown/black)  
✓ Medieval sagas describe Vikings with red hair at notable frequency  
✓ Inheritance: two copies = red hair, one copy = carrier  
✓ Highest concentration in Northern Europe + Celtic regions  

**DO NOT:**
- Invent percentages or studies
- Write "scientists were shocked" or vague hedging
- Compare to unproven traits like Morton's toe
- Tease a reveal that doesn't come
