# Working preferences for this repo

## Delivering documents to Gareth — IMPORTANT

**Never hand over a `.md` file as the deliverable.** Gareth cannot open Markdown
files, either in the chat panel or after downloading them. Sending one means the
work does not arrive.

Instead, for any document, brief, plan, checklist or package:

1. Write the content as a **self-contained HTML file**.
2. Publish it with the **Artifact** tool and give him the link.

The Artifact is the deliverable. Do this without being asked, every time.

Keeping a `.md` copy in the repo as the source of truth is fine and encouraged —
just never present it as the thing to open.

## Channel context

- **Channel:** Signs of the Norse — Discover Your Viking Ancestry
- **Format:** faceless voiceover over stock and AI-generated footage
- **Audience:** ~1,500 subscribers interested in ancestry and physical traits
- **Series:** a "trait cluster" — each video investigates one physical trait and
  its link to Northern European ancestry. Videos cross-link to each other.

**Published so far:** Viking Finger (Dupuytren's) → Viking Toe (Morton's toe) →
Red Hair. **Next:** Blue Eyes.

## House rules for video content

- Never invent percentages, studies, or researcher names.
- Separate settled science from contested interpretation, and attribute the
  contested parts rather than asserting them.
- Never overclaim that a trait *proves* Viking descent — traits indicate
  Northern European ancestry. Overclaiming gets ancestry channels written off.
- No "scientists were shocked" phrasing, no teasing a reveal that never arrives.
