## Headline finding

The channel's decline began **while uploads were still going out**, not after they stopped —
videos 1–3 caught the new-channel boost, videos 4–9 collapsed. The problem was content
shape, not consistency.

The niche has since filled up: ten of the twenty channels surveyed launched after the last
ISBB upload. But the format that won is not the one the channel was making — 45–90 minute
AI features and 10–30 minute researched documentaries, not 4-minute clips. One channel
created the same month as ISBB now sits at 112K subscribers on seven videos.

Recommendation: restart rather than resume, at feature length, in first person.

## Data caveats

View and subscriber counts above ~1,000 are rounded by the source APIs, competitor start
dates are bucketed by month, and revenue figures are AdSense estimates that exclude
sponsorship and affiliate income. See the Method section of the brief for details.
